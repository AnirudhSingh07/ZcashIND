## Core terms

**ZEC** — the unit of Zcash. What you hold and send.

**Shielded** — a payment where the sender, receiver, and amount are hidden from the public chain, verified with cryptography.

**Transparent** — a payment where those details are public, like Bitcoin.

**Unified Address (UA)** — one modern address that can receive from anyone and prefers the shielded pool. Share this when someone asks for your address.

**Seed phrase** — the list of words that backs up your wallet. Whoever has it controls the funds. Keep it offline and private. We never ask for it.

**Wallet** — the app that holds your ZEC and keys. Zashi, Zingo and Zodl are mobile options.

**Zero-knowledge proof** — the math that lets the network confirm a shielded payment is valid without seeing its details. The "zk" in Zcash.

**Testnet** — a practice network where coins have no value, used to learn safely.

**Mainnet** — the real Zcash network with real ZEC.

## Community terms

**IRL meetup** — an in-person Zcash India gathering. Four or more people, one real conversation.

**Node #** — the sequential number we assign each verified meetup in a city. Your city's first verified meetup is Node #01.

**New-city activation** — the first ever verified meetup in a city. It lights up a brand new pin on the map.

**Contributor** — someone recognised for consistent, real impact in the community. Not automatic — see [Contributors](/contributors).
