::lead Financial privacy isn't about hiding. It's the same everyday dignity you already expect — applied to money. Here's why it matters for real people in India.

## Remittances

Millions of families in India receive money from relatives working abroad. Every transfer can leave a detailed trail across intermediaries. Private payments mean a family's income and relationships aren't an open book to every service in the chain.

## Freelancers and creators

If you do cross-border work — design, code, writing, content — you often receive payments from clients around the world. Shielded payments mean your rates, your client list, and your running balance aren't broadcast publicly for competitors or scrapers to read.

## Students

Students experimenting with crypto shouldn't have their entire financial history permanently public because they once received a small amount. Privacy lets people learn and participate without a permanent public ledger following them for life.

## Merchants

Small merchants who accept digital payments deserve the same confidentiality a cash till gives them. Private payments keep daily takings and supplier relationships from being visible to anyone who knows their address. Merchants across India are already listed on [ZecMap](https://zecmap.com/map).

## The principle

Privacy is the default in most of life. You don't announce your salary. You don't publish your bank statement. Choosing privacy for a payment is normal and legitimate.

## What this is not
