::lead Zcash is digital cash. Like Bitcoin, it lives on a public blockchain. Unlike Bitcoin, it can hide the details of a payment so only you and the person you paid can see them.

## Cash, but digital

Think about physical cash. When you hand someone a ₹500 note, there's no permanent public record of who you are, who they are, and what you bought. Zcash brings that everyday privacy to digital money.

The unit is **ZEC**. You hold it in a wallet on your phone. You can send it to anyone in the world with an internet connection.

## The key idea: shielded payments

Zcash has two kinds of addresses:

- **Transparent**: works like Bitcoin. Amounts and addresses are visible on the public chain.
- **Shielded**: uses cryptography (zero-knowledge proofs) so the network can confirm a payment is valid **without revealing** the sender, receiver, or amount.

Modern Zcash wallets use a **unified address** that prefers the shielded pool. In practice: you get privacy by default, and you don't have to think about the plumbing.

## What Zcash is not

- It is **not** a way to break the law. Privacy is a normal right, and this community does not help with anything illegal.
- It is **not** a get-rich scheme. We never tell you a price will go up. This is about a tool, not a bet.
- It is **not** run by Zcash India. We're a community that teaches and organises. The protocol is open and global.
