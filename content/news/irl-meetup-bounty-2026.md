::lead We're opening the **Zcash India IRL Meetup Bounty** for September 2026. Host a small, real meetup — get it verified — and earn a spot on the map, a share of the prize pool, and a path to becoming a contributor.

This is not about big numbers or influencers. It's about real conversations in real rooms. A café. A hostel. A college classroom. Four people who leave knowing a little more about financial privacy than they did an hour ago.

## The essentials

- **Prize pool: $150** — 1st $40, 2nd $25, 3rd $20, five outstanding meetups $10 each, five new-city activations $3 each.
- **Minimum to qualify:** 4 attendees, at least 2 new to Zcash, 20+ minutes, 3 photos (one a group photo), Zcash / Zcash India branding visible, and a real conversation — not a photo-op.
- **Window:** September 2026. (Confirm exact dates on the [bounty page](/bounties/irl).)

## What counts

Colleges, cafés / chai meetups, hostels and homes, coworking spaces, offices, and public spaces. Formats can be a privacy talk, a Zcash intro, an Ironwood session, a quiz or game, a dev session, chai & privacy, or a college discussion.

## How to enter

1. Read the [bounty rules and judging](/bounties/irl).
2. Grab the [host kit](/host) — a 20–40 minute run of show and a photo checklist.
3. Host your meetup during the window.
4. [Submit it](/bounties/irl/submit) with your photos. Our team reviews every submission.

Participation alone doesn't guarantee contributor status. Impact does. **Put your city on the map.**
