::lead September 2026 is a big month for Zcash India. Official meetups are locked in for Indore, Udaipur and Vadodara, the online **Zcash India Live** series continues, and our first IRL meetup bounty opens.

Over the last few months the community has grown from a handful of people in a Telegram group to real rooms full of students, freelancers and merchants asking honest questions about financial privacy. That's the whole point. **Impact > numbers.**

## What happened

- **Campus workshops** ran across Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat, walking students through shielded vs transparent transactions and a first testnet-style transaction.
- **Chai & privacy** sessions in Ahmedabad brought freelancers together to talk about receiving cross-border payments privately.
- The **Mumbai light client** came online, giving the community more resilient infrastructure to build on.
- Merchants across India kept getting listed on [ZecMap](https://zecmap.com/map) — we point people there rather than building a second database.

## What's next

The [IRL meetup bounty](/bounties/irl) opens for September 2026. If you've ever wanted to host, this is the moment. You don't need to be an expert or an influencer. You need four people, one real conversation, and a willingness to put your city on the map.

- Read [Start Here](/learn/start-here) if you're brand new.
- Grab the [host kit](/host) if you want to run a meetup.
- Say hi on [Telegram](https://t.me/ZcashIN).

One conversation. One meetup. One city at a time.
